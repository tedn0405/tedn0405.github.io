Method

Manning's equation in SI units: Q = (1/n) · A · R2/3 · S1/2, with A = πD²/4 and R = D/4 for a full pipe.

For a partly full pipe, the calculator solves for the water depth whose flow equals your design flow, assuming n is constant at all depths.

Good to know
  • A circular pipe carries its maximum flow at about 94% depth, roughly 1.08 × Qfull. Flows above Qfull are flagged as surcharged here, which is how most design criteria treat them.
  • The web version solves depth exactly. The Excel version reads it from a 1% table and rounds up, so it can show a slightly deeper flow.
