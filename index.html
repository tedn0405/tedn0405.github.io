What I design

Anatomy of a wet pond

A wet pond treats runoff, protects the receiving stream from erosion and controls peak flows, each with its own slice of storage. This is the kind of facility I size, grade and take through approval.

My approach

Dry pond with a DICB outlet

Where the site allows it, this is the facility I prefer to design. The pond drains completely between storms, and the outlet control sits in a standard ditch inlet catch basin at the toe of the slope, where a crew can inspect it from the surface.

Background in water resources

Western University · 2020–2022

Probabilistic assessment of tornado and flash flood events

Graduate research using copula-based statistics in ArcGIS Pro and R to assess flood risk for infrastructure resilience planning across Canada.

Winner, Civil Engineering Three-Minute Thesis (3MT). Presented at the AGU annual conference.

Peer-reviewed publication · 2023

Ngui, Y.D., Najafi, M.R., de Souza, C., and Sills, D. (2023). Probabilistic assessment of concurrent tornado and storm-related flash flood events. International Journal of Climatology, 43, 4231–4247.

Tornadoes and flash floods can hit the same place at the same time, and their safety advice conflicts: shelter underground, or move to high ground. The paper uses copula models to estimate how often the two occur together across Canada. The link between tornado wind speed and rainfall was strongest in Saskatchewan.
